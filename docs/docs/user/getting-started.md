# Getting Started

Welcome to Canopy. This page explains, in plain terms, what Canopy is, how to
get it running, and what you can do with it today.

## What is Canopy?

Canopy is a self-hostable platform for **geospatial data and maps**. You run it
on your own server, behind your own domain name, and it gives your team:

- **Accounts and teams** — invite people, group them into teams, and control who
  can do what through roles.
- **Atlases and maps** — organize maps into collections ("atlases") and share
  them with specific teams.
- **Notifications** — users are notified in real time about relevant events
  (for example, when their permissions change).
- **Direct database access** — advanced users can be granted their own secure
  login to the underlying geospatial database.

:::note Under active development
Canopy is a work in progress. Some parts of the interface (interactive map
display and the visual map editor) are **not available yet** and appear as
"work in progress" screens. Account management, teams, atlases, notifications
and profile settings are the features you can use today.
:::

## How to get Canopy running

Canopy is not a hosted service you sign up for — you deploy your own instance.
If you (or a colleague) need to put it online, follow the friendly, step-by-step
[Deployment guide](./deployment). It's written for non-specialists.

Once an instance is running, it's available at your own address, e.g.
`https://maps.yourcompany.com`.

## First steps as a user

1. **Open your Canopy address** in a modern browser (Chrome, Firefox, Safari or
   Edge).
2. **Create an account**, or **log in** if an administrator already made one for
   you. New sign-ups confirm their email address via a link (make sure to check
   your inbox).
3. **Set up your profile** from the profile page — you can change your name,
   email and password there.
4. If your administrator gave you **database access**, activate it from your
   profile to receive your personal database login.

## For administrators

If you deployed Canopy, your first login uses the built-in `admin` account.
**Change its password immediately** and remove the demo accounts — see step 7 of
the [Deployment guide](./deployment#7-secure-your-first-login-important). From
the **Admin** section you can then manage users, roles, teams and atlases.
